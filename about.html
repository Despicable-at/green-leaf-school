About Green Leaf

Growth begins with care.

A school community in Atimatim, Edwenase, committed to child-centred learning.

Our philosophy

Every child deserves room to grow.

We believe learning should begin with the child: their curiosity, their strengths and their readiness to take the next step.

At Green Leaf School, care and learning belong together. A conducive learning environment, well-groomed teachers and opportunities to participate beyond classroom lessons help create a considered school experience.

Our community welcomes children from Creche, Nursery and Primary through JHS.

A thoughtful beginning can open up a lifetime of possibility.

What guides us

Purpose, direction, practice.
